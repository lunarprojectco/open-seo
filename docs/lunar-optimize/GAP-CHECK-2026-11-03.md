# Lunar Optimize: the owner's UX review checked against what is live

Date: 2026-11-03. Live: optimize.projectlunar.ai, open-seo branch `lunar-optimize-seo-engine` at `b1dcab4` plus the release-kit scripts (`optimize-release-kit`, 2 commits, config only), running from `/opt/lunar-optimize/app` on the Studio box behind nginx on 127.0.0.1:3601.

Source of the review: the owner's pasted "Complete UI/UX, Feature Architecture & Frontend Optimization Analysis" (November 2026). Its own boundary applies: it is a product-level spec, not an audit of this app. This file is the audit.

## What was checked, and how

- The public pages, in a browser at 1440 and 390: `/`, `/welcome` (serves the same landing), `/sign-in`, `/subscribe` (redirects to sign-in).
- The signed-in app, from source only: every route under `src/routes`, the sidebar (`src/client/navigation/items.ts`, `src/client/components/Sidebar.tsx`), the shell (`src/client/layout/AppShell.tsx`), and the feature folders.
- **Not seen:** any signed-in screen rendered. The workspace is invite-only (`LUNAR_OPTIMIZE_ALLOWED_EMAILS`), and no test account exists. A local run with `AUTH_MODE=local_noauth` can render them for the next pass.

## The public landing: fine, and more honest than the review assumes

The landing already does what the review asks of the product's voice: "Illustrative interface, no sample rankings or traffic claims" under the hero card; "Data availability and freshness are shown in the product" in the footer; "Provider data and usage credits vary by feature". Six capability cards (keyword research, rank tracking, competitor insights, backlinks, site audits, AI visibility), a three-step start, and one plan at $99/month with $20 of metered credits. No defects at either width, no sideways scroll, no console errors.

One state to know about: "Enrollment will open after billing setup is complete." The Create your workspace button leads to sign-in, and sign-up is invite-only. That is a launch blocker on the business side, not a UX defect.

## The signed-in app: the review's main criticism lands here

The sidebar is OpenSEO's, twelve items for one project: Dashboard, Keyword Research, Content Score, Saved Keywords, Rank Tracking, GSC Insights, Domain Overview, Backlinks, Site Audit, Brand Lookup, Prompt Explorer, plus a Connect group with AI & MCP. Every research tool is a permanent navigation item, which is the first row of the review's table of things to avoid.

## Two facts the review does not account for

1. **Optimize is a separate application from Compass and Studio.** It is a TanStack Start app on workerd with its own auth (`api/auth`), its own Postgres (drizzle), and its own billing placeholder. Compass, Content Factory and Claw are in mission-control behind Clerk. "Move from a Compass finding into Optimize and back without losing state" means single sign-on and shared tenant context across two apps. That is the real P0 dependency, and it is not a frontend change.
2. **Sign-up is invite-only and billing is a placeholder.** Until enrollment opens, every signed-in journey in the review is a journey for the owner and invited testers only.

## P0, proposed: repair and consolidate inside the existing shell

Done inside this repo, on this branch, no new application, no new data store. Each item has a check.

1. **Five local destinations instead of twelve.** Overview, Intelligence, Opportunities, Audit, Settings. Keyword Research, Saved, Domain, Backlinks, Brand Lookup, Prompt Explorer and GSC Insights become views inside Intelligence, reached by a command search and tabs (the existing `search-tabs`). Nothing is removed; every old URL keeps working with a redirect. Check: a signed-in user reaches every existing tool within two clicks from Overview; old links 302 to the new place.
2. **An Opportunity object, derived, not scored.** `opportunity` rows built from three sources that already exist: audit issues by severity, striking-distance keywords from GSC, content-score gaps. Fields as the review lists them, with `impact` left empty when the inputs cannot support it, and no proprietary score. Check: every opportunity shows its source rows, timestamp and limitation; a fixture run produces the same list twice.
3. **One source-and-freshness contract on metric cards.** A `MetricCard` that always shows provider, collected-at and sample/observed, and refuses to show a date range it does not have data for. Check: a card with stale data says so; a card with no data is visibly different from a measured zero.
4. **State audit of every signed-in screen** against the review's list (connecting, loading, no data, insufficient sample, stale, disconnected, failed sync, missing permission). Done with a local run and screenshots at 1440 and 390, recorded in `docs/lunar-optimize/STATES-2026-11.md`. Check: the file lists each screen and each state with a screenshot or "not reachable".
5. **Overview answers "what changed":** last 7 and 28 days of GSC clicks and impressions, tracked-keyword movement, audit issues opened and closed, with the freshness contract. No leads or revenue on it until a CRM source exists; those tiles show "not connected", not an invented figure.

Left for P1 or later, and said so: the cross-app SSO and shared tenant context (needs an owner decision on identity: Clerk for both, or a signed handoff); Experiments; Activity and agent execution; local rank grids; website editing and conversion intelligence (other Lunar apps).

## What to decide

- Approve P0 as the five items above, or cut it to 1 and 3 first.
- Identity between mission-control and Optimize: Clerk for both, or a signed handoff from Studio. The "Compass to Optimize and back" acceptance cannot be met without it.
- Whether enrollment (billing) opens before or after P0 ships.
